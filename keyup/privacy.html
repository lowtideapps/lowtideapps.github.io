Keyup Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version

Keyup doesn't collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Voice and chat

Your voice and messages go only to the Mumble server you choose to connect to. That server's owner sets their own rules.

Stored on your phone

Favorites, settings, chat history, saved photos and your identity certificate stay on your phone. Saved passwords are encrypted with a key that never leaves it.

Microphone

Used only while you're connected to a server or running the audio check.

Crash and bug reports

Only sent if you tap Send, through an app you choose (for example email). You can read them first.

Tips

Handled entirely by Google Play. Keyup never sees your payment details.

Children

Keyup isn't directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use Report a bug in the app's Connection screen, or open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
